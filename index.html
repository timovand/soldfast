Questions sellers ask us

How much less than market value will you offer?

Typically 20–30% below what an agent would list at. In return you pay no commission, no transfer costs, no repairs, no compliance certificates, and no further months of bond and rates while you wait for a buyer.

What kind of property do you buy?

Houses, townhouses, flats and plots anywhere in Gauteng, in any condition — tenanted, vacant, damaged, unfinished or tied up in an estate.

Can you buy if the bank has already started legal action?

Yes, as long as the property hasn't been sold at auction yet. We deal with the bank's attorneys directly and the arrears are handled as part of the sale.

Do I have to move out immediately?

No. We agree the occupation date with you. Some sellers stay on for a few weeks after the deposit is paid while they find their next home.

Which areas do you buy in?

All of Gauteng.
