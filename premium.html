What a private sale should feel like

Discretion

No board, no listing, no open house

One viewing by the person who signs the offer. Your neighbours and colleagues need never know the home was for sale.

Certainty

Cash, not a bond application

Roughly one in four conventional sales collapses on finance. Ours cannot. The offer you accept is the amount you receive.

Pace

Days, on your timetable

A written offer within 24 hours, funds within 24 hours of signing, and a move-out date set by you rather than by a buyer.

Every figure, in writing, before you decide

Most cash buyers quote a single number and hope you don't ask how they reached it. Our valuation letter sets out the independent market value, our purchase price, and each balance we settle on your behalf, so the amount paid to you is unambiguous.

The letter is valid for seven days and carries no obligation.

Questions

How far below market value is the offer?

Typically 10–20%. Against that you pay no commission, no repairs, no compliance certificates, and carry no bond, rates or levies for the months a listing would take.

Who holds the money?

An independent conveyancing attorney, in trust, exactly as in a conventional transfer. You receive the firm's details before you sign anything.

Is the viewing really the only visit?

Yes. The buyer who views the property is the person who signs the offer. There are no follow-up inspections or show days.

When must we move out?

The occupation date is agreed with you and written into the offer to purchase. Many sellers remain for several weeks after payment.

Which areas do you purchase in?

All of Gauteng, including Pretoria East, Sandton and the northern suburbs, Centurion, Midrand and the East Rand.
