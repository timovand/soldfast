Privacy notice

Last updated 6 November 2026

SoldFast ("we", "us") buys residential property in Gauteng. This notice explains how we handle personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you contact us through this website, WhatsApp or telephone we may collect your name, contact details, the address and details of the property you want to sell, information about bonds, arrears and occupancy, and your reason and timeframe for selling. We collect only what we need to assess the property and make you an offer.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and arrange a viewing
  • To assess the property and prepare a conditional and final offer
  • To prepare an offer to purchase and related transfer documents if you accept
  • To keep records required by law, including FICA

Who we share it with

We share your information only with parties needed to complete a sale: conveyancing attorneys, your bond holder or municipality where settlement of arrears forms part of the sale, and our investment partners where a property is transferred to them. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a sale are deleted within 12 months. Records of completed transactions are kept for the period required by law, generally five years.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our processing. Email info@soldfast.co.za. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Information Regulator at inforeg.org.za.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses basic analytics to understand how visitors use it. No advertising cookies are set without your consent.

Security

Your information is stored on access-controlled systems and transmitted over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the Information Regulator if a breach affects you.
