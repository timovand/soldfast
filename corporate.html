Frequently asked questions

How is the offer calculated?

Independent market value less a fixed percentage, typically 10–20%, with every balance we settle listed. The sample letter above is the format you receive.

How do I know the money will be paid?

Funds are released by an independent conveyancing attorney from a trust account, exactly as in a conventional sale. You receive the firm's details before signing.

Can you buy if the bank has already issued summons?

Yes, provided the property has not yet been sold at auction. We liaise with the bank's attorneys and settle the arrears as part of the sale.

When do I need to vacate?

The occupation date is agreed with you and recorded in the offer to purchase.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Gauteng: Johannesburg, Pretoria, East Rand, West Rand and the Vaal Triangle.
